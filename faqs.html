Frequently asked questions

Everything about ordering, shipping and care. Edit these in faqs.html.

Where do you ship?

We currently ship within Canada only. This is due to our focus on serving Canadian customers and the complexity of international shipping due to tariffs.

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled by invoices. We will charge your band when you authorise us to in order to finalize the purchase.

How long does delivery take?

Most in-stock items ship within 1 to 3 business days. Made-to-order bagpipes and kilts take longer; we'll confirm timing by email.

Can I return an item?

Unused items in original condition can be returned within 30 days. Custom or made-to-measure items (e.g. kilts) and instruments are non-returnable.

Do you set up or tune bagpipes before shipping?

Yes - pipes are checked and set up before they go out - but only if you want us to. Add a note at checkout or contact us with any requests.
